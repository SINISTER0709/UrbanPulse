# Municipal SOP: Stormwater Drainage, Sewers & Urban Flooding

## 1. Flash Flooding, Submerged Underpasses & Missing Manholes (Critical)
Immediate response SLA: under 1 hour. For flooded railway underbridges, waist/chest-deep road flooding, or missing manhole covers hidden under murky water, dispatch traffic barricades and emergency dewatering pumps immediately. Place bright tripod warning markers over open sewer shafts before installing temporary heavy-duty covers. If toxic hydrogen sulfide (H2S) sewer gas or worker fainting is reported, prohibit unventilated entry and deploy mechanical blowers and safety rescue gear.

## 2. High-Severity Sewage Backups & School/Hospital Waterlogging
Raw sewage overflowing into homes, clinics, or school compounds and knee-deep arterial waterlogging require super-sucker / jetting machine deployment within 6-12 hours. Clear plastic-choked culvert mouths manually with safety hooks and restore stormwater pumping station power/motors.

## 3. Medium & Routine Desilting
Blocked side gutters, cracked residential drain slabs, and recurring ankle-deep puddles carry a 48-72 hour desilting and slab-replacement SLA. Pre-monsoon gully cleaning, minor silt buildup, and rattling flush manhole lids are scheduled into the ward preventive maintenance calendar.
