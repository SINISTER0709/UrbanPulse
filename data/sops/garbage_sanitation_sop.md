# Municipal SOP: Garbage & Sanitation Management

## 1. Hazardous, Biomedical & Burning Waste (Critical)
Immediate response SLA: under 2 hours. For dumped hospital syringes, red/yellow biomedical bags, leaking chemical drums, or burning landfill/dhalao piles near schools, hospitals, or fuel stations, coordinate immediately with Fire Services and the Pollution Control / Biomedical Waste authorized vendor. Cordon off the site to prevent ragpicker or animal exposure; never mix biomedical waste into regular municipal compactor trucks.

## 2. High-Severity Accumulations, Carcasses & Choked Public Toilets
Dead animal carcasses on public roads must be lifted by the dedicated carcass-recovery van within 6 hours, followed by lime/bleaching-powder disinfection. Multi-day rotting waste mounds near clinics, schools, or markets and completely choked public toilet blocks require mechanized loader clearance and pressure washing within 12-24 hours.

## 3. Routine Collection & Overflowing Bins (Medium/Low)
Overflowing community bins (dhalaos) and missed door-to-door collection routes must be cleared within 24-48 hours and logged against the ward sanitation supervisor's beat chart. Minor litter, fallen dry leaves, and public toilet soap/mopping requests are routed to the next morning's regular sweeping shift.
