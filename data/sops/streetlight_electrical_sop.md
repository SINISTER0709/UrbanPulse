# Municipal SOP: Streetlight & Electrical Hazards

## 1. Live Wires, Transformer Fires & Electrocution Risk (Critical)
Immediate response SLA: under 30 minutes. Contact the local DISCOM / Electrical Grid control room immediately to isolate the feeder line before field crews approach. Never step into standing water near a fallen streetlight pole or snapped 11kV/440V conductor. Establish a 15-meter safety perimeter and coordinate with Fire Services for burning transformers or feeder pillars.

## 2. High-Severity Outages, Sparking Equipment & Exposed Live Boxes
Complete streetlight blackouts on arterial flyovers, hospital approaches, or school/college corridors require restoration within 24 hours. Open feeder pillar boxes with bare live busbars, leaning electric poles (>20 degrees), or electrified metal lamp posts must be made safe and locked/insulated within 4-6 hours.

## 3. Medium & Routine Streetlight Maintenance
Clusters of 3-5 dead residential streetlights, broken timer controllers, or sagging insulated service cables carry a 48-72 hour repair SLA. Single flickering LEDs, daytime-burning lamps, faded pole stencils, or dusty solar panels are scheduled into the weekly ward electrical beat.
