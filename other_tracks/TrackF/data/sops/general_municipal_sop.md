# Municipal SOP: General Civic Hazards & Inter-Departmental Coordination

## 1. Structural Collapse, Unstable Hoardings & Rabid Animal Attacks (Critical)
Immediate response SLA: under 1 hour. For actively tilting commercial billboards, collapsing municipal boundary walls, unbarricaded deep construction pits where vehicles/pedestrians have fallen, or confirmed rabid dog attacks outside schools/hospitals, immediately alert Disaster Management, Police, and the Municipal Veterinary / Enforcement squad. Cordon off the fall zone and halt adjacent pedestrian and vehicular movement.

## 2. High-Severity Fallen Trees, Encroachments & Vector Outbreaks
Large uprooted trees blocking hospital/arterial roads (verify no entangled live electrical cables first; if present, hand off to Electrical/DISCOM first) require Horticulture chainsaw crews within 4 hours. Encroachments blocking fire-tender or hospital gates require immediate Enforcement clearance. Confirmed dengue/malaria clusters require anti-larval spraying and thermal fogging within 24 hours.

## 3. Medium & Routine Civic Requests
Late-night loudspeaker violations (refer to Police/PCB after 10 PM), stray cattle herds on roads, overgrown vegetation hiding traffic signs, broken park swings, and faded street signboards carry a 3-7 day SLA through the respective Horticulture, Veterinary, or Enforcement desk.
