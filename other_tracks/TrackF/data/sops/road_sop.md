# Municipal SOP: Road Damage & Pavement Safety (Public Works Department)

## 1. Critical Road Collapses & Sinkholes
Immediate response SLA: under 2 hours. Dispatch rapid structural engineering team and traffic police liaison. Erect reflective barricades and night hazard beacons at least 25 meters upstream of sinkholes, collapsed culverts, or cracked flyover slabs. Divert heavy buses and ambulances away from undermined carriageways before any temporary filling begins.

## 2. High-Severity Potholes Near Schools & Hospitals
High-severity school-zone and hospital-access potholes require warning cones and barricading within 4 hours, and cold-mix or hot-mix bitumen patching by the Public Works road crew within 24 hours. Prioritize approaches within 250 meters of schools, hospitals, and emergency stations. Schedule heavy compaction outside peak school drop-off hours (7:30-9:00 AM and 1:30-3:00 PM) while maintaining safe pedestrian passage.

## 3. Medium & Routine Road Maintenance
Medium pothole clusters, sunken utility trenches, and broken footpath paver blocks carry a 72-hour repair SLA. Verify whether a telecom or utility contractor performed recent excavation; log contractor liability before resurfacing. Low-severity faded zebra crossings, minor hairline cracks, and worn kerb paint are batched into the weekly ward maintenance schedule (7-14 days).
