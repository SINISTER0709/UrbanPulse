# Municipal SOP: Drinking Water Supply & Quality

## 1. Contamination Outbreaks, Chlorine Leaks & Major Trunk Bursts (Critical)
Immediate response SLA: under 1 hour. If sewage-contaminated or chemically tainted drinking water is reported with resident illness, immediately shut off the affected zonal distribution valve, issue a boil/do-not-drink advisory, collect water quality samples, and dispatch emergency potable water tankers. For chlorine gas leaks at booster stations, evacuate adjacent lanes and alert HazMat/Fire teams. Major trunk pipeline blowouts (12-inch+) require immediate upstream sluice valve closure to prevent road undermining and hospital/fire-hydrant pressure loss.

## 2. High-Severity Outages & Broken Distribution Mains
Complete water cutoffs lasting >48 hours, burst 6-10 inch distribution mains, or outages affecting hospitals, schools, and dense settlements require emergency municipal water tankers within 6 hours and pipeline clamping/repair within 24 hours.

## 3. Medium & Routine Supply Issues
Low line pressure, broken community handpumps, leaking 2-inch service pipes, and faulty public standposts carry a 48-72 hour repair SLA. Minor washer drips, slightly shifted supply timings, or fogged meter covers are logged for routine zonal plumber visits within 7 days.
